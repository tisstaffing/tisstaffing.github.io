Our process

How we run every search

  1. 01

    Dedicated recruitment team

    One named consultant and a support researcher own your search from brief to start date.

  2. 02

    Position definition & alignment review

    We agree the role, the compensation range and what success looks like before a single advert goes live.

  3. 03

    Deep-dive personal candidate interviews

    Every shortlisted candidate is interviewed by us first, covering skills, motivation and cultural fit.

  4. 04

    Tailored market strategy & outreach messaging

    We map the market and shape how the opportunity is pitched so it lands with the people you actually want.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions hiring managers and candidates ask us most before starting a search.

01

TIS Staffing is a technology, finance, executive and operations recruitment agency that finds, screens and places permanent, contract and interim professionals. We work with employers across the US and Canada, running each search from brief through to start date.

02

We recruit across technology and engineering, finance and accounting, retained executive search, and operations including HR, payroll and administration. That covers everyone from analysts and developers to controllers, managers and senior leaders.

03

TIS Staffing has offices in Dallas and Toronto. From those two locations we support employers across the US and Canada.

04
How much do staffing agencies charge?

The employer pays the fee, never the candidate. Direct-hire and contract models are priced differently, and TIS Staffing also offers a placement guarantee.

Read the full staffing fee guide
05

An executive shortlist usually lands within three weeks. Timelines for other roles depend on the brief, and one named consultant owns each search from start to finish.

06
Can I request an employment verification?

Yes — current and former placements can request a Verification of Employment directly from our HR team. Victoria Wright, HR Manager, handles these requests and replies with the dates, titles and assignment history we hold.
