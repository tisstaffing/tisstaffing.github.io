Employment verification

Verification of Employment

Need to confirm current or previous employment with TIS Staffing? Send your request to our HR team and we will work through it with you.

What to include in your request

Employee authorization

A signed release from the employee, so we can share their details with you.

Full legal name

The name used during employment, plus any former or maiden name on record.

Dates or role

The employment dates or job title you are verifying, if you already have them.

Reply contact

Your company name, plus the person and address we should send the response to.

We only release information the employee has authorized. Requests that arrive without authorization will be sent back for a signed release.
