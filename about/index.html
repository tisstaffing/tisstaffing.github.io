Our story

Built by recruiters who got tired of the old way.

TIS Staffing started in 2004 with one desk, one phone and a stubborn belief that hiring should feel like a partnership rather than a transaction. Two decades later that belief still runs everything we do.

We are a boutique agency, which means you get a named consultant who knows your business, not a rotating queue of account managers. We only take on searches we genuinely believe we can fill, and we say so when we cannot.

Today our teams place professionals across technology, finance, leadership and HR from two offices in North America — always with the same promise: real candidates, properly vetted, and a process you can actually trust.

The principles behind every search

Honest by default

Speed with rigor

Shortlists land in days, not months, because every candidate is properly interviewed before we send them your way.

How TIS grew up

From a two-person startup in Dallas to a two-office agency trusted by companies across the US and Canada. Here are the moments that shaped us.

2004

TIS Staffing is founded

Margaret Ellison opens a two-person office in Dallas with a simple idea: recruit like a partner, not a vendor.

2009

First international office

We open in Toronto to support clients expanding across North American borders.

2014

5,000 placements

Our finance and admin practices hit a combined 5,000 successful placements.

2019

Technology practice launched

A dedicated engineering and data division is built, led by consultants who code.

2024

12,000 placements, 2 offices

Two decades in, we place talent across Dallas and Toronto.
