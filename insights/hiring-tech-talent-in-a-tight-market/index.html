Hiring tech talent in a tight market

How to scope senior engineering roles so the right people actually apply — instead of watching your job ad gather dust.

Daniel Osei

Managing Director, Technology

Every engineering leader we work with says the same thing: the pipeline is empty. But when we read their job spec, it is usually a list of twelve technologies and no clear reason why a great engineer should care.

Fewer requirements, sharper ones

You do not need a candidate who matches every bullet. You need someone who can do the actual job. Split your list into genuine must-haves and nice-to-haves, then cut the must-haves down until only three or four remain.

When we rewrote one client brief from fourteen requirements to four, qualified applications tripled in a week.

Sell the problem, not the perk list

Strong engineers are not won by table football and free snacks. They are won by hard problems, talented teammates and a clear sense that their work matters. Lead your ad with the challenge, not the benefits page.

Move at their speed

Good engineers are interviewing in three places at once. If your loop takes three weeks and your competitor closes in ten days, you will lose — regardless of how good the role is.

Compress the process, decide quickly, and keep the candidate warm throughout. In a tight market, speed is a feature.
